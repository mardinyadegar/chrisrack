Returns / RMA

A straightforward process for the rare component that fails.

Every rack is burned in before it ships, but hardware fails in the field. Our returns process is built to get a defective component evaluated and resolved without holding your deployment down any longer than necessary.

01

Submit Request

Contact your account manager or support with the rack serial number and a description of the failure.

02

RMA Number Issued

We issue an RMA number and shipping instructions, or dispatch a technician for on-site evaluation.

03

Evaluation

Our technicians evaluate the returned component and confirm root cause before repair or replacement.

04

Repair or Replace

Warranty items are repaired or replaced at no charge; out-of-warranty items are quoted before work begins.

Before You Ship Anything

Call first — most issues don't require a return

A large share of field calls are resolved over the phone with a remote diagnostic check or a firmware fix. Support will only issue an RMA once a hardware failure is confirmed, so you're not shipping a component back for a configuration change.
