The Coreframe Standard

Configured. Verified. Powered-On.

Three commitments that hold regardless of rack count, component mix, or ship date.

C

Configured

Every rack is built to the released BOM and elevation drawing, with firmware and BIOS settings staged to your image before it ever leaves the bench.

V

Verified

Cable maps and asset tags are checked against the design twice — once at integration, once at final QA — not sampled, every rack.

P

Powered-On

Every system is burned in under load before it ships, so the first time it powers on in your data center isn’t the first time it’s ever powered on at all.
