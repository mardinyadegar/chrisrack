Integration

One rack. Every discipline, coordinated.

Rack integration means compute, storage, and network all land in the same cabinet, cabled and tested by the same team that engineered the elevation. That coordination is where lead times — and install-day surprises — either get controlled or get away from you.

Our Process

Six stages, one schedule

The same released schedule governs procurement and integration, so a change on the BOM doesn't quietly become a surprise on the ship date.

01

Design Intake

Elevation drawings, specs, and BOM reviewed against your standards and available lead times.

02

Engineering Release

Rack elevation, power/cooling load check, and cable schematic review before integration is scheduled.

03

Rack & Stack

Server, storage, and switch mounting, PDU installation, and rail kit assembly on the integration floor.

04

Cabling & Imaging

Structured cabling, labeling, firmware staging, and OS or hypervisor imaging to the released spec.

05

Burn-In & QA

72-hour load testing, thermal monitoring, and cable map verification against the design.

06

Ship & Deploy

Crated and shipped on schedule, with optional white-glove install and rack-in-place service.
