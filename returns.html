Returns / RMA

A straightforward process for the rare defect that slips through.

Every enclosure is checked against its design before it ships, but damage happens in transit and defects happen in the field. Our returns process is built to get an issue evaluated and resolved without holding your deployment down any longer than necessary.

01

Submit Request

Contact us with the order or serial reference and a description of the issue.

02

RMA Number Issued

We issue an RMA number and shipping instructions, or arrange an on-site evaluation.

03

Evaluation

We evaluate the returned item and confirm root cause before repair or replacement.

04

Repair or Replace

Warranty items are repaired or replaced at no charge; out-of-warranty items are quoted before work begins.

Before You Ship Anything

Call first — most issues don't require a return

A shipping scuff or a missing accessory is often resolved over email. We'll only issue an RMA once an issue is confirmed, so you're not shipping a cabinet back for something that didn't need it.
