Company

We design and build the cabinets AI infrastructure runs in.

Rax By Design manufactures custom rack enclosures and cabinetry for AI, GPU, and high-density compute environments. We work from your elevation and workload requirements — not a catalog SKU — and partner with trusted integration specialists like Rackworx to deliver a rack that's ready to power on.

Approach

Built for a new generation of compute density

Traditional rack enclosures were designed around yesterday's power and thermal envelopes. AI and GPU clusters change the assumptions: heavier chassis, denser power distribution, tighter thermal margins, and cabling requirements that don't fit a stock 42U frame.

Rax By Design exists to close that gap — engineering and fabricating cabinetry to the actual requirements of the workload it will house, then handing off cleanly to your integration team, or ours, through our partnership with Rackworx.
