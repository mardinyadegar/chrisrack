Manufacturing

One enclosure. Every discipline, coordinated.

Building a cabinet that's ready for high-density AI compute means structural, thermal, and power design all have to agree before metal gets cut. That coordination is where lead times — and install-day surprises — either get controlled or get away from you.

Our Process

Six stages, one schedule

The same released schedule governs engineering and fabrication, so a change on the drawing doesn't quietly become a surprise on the ship date.

01

Design Intake

Elevation drawings, specs, and workload requirements reviewed against your standards and timeline.

02

Engineering Release

Structural load, thermal, and power distribution review before fabrication is scheduled.

03

Fabrication

Frame cutting, forming, and welding to the released drawing.

04

Finishing & Assembly

Powder coat or plating, then doors, rails, PDU brackets, and cable pathways assembled.

05

QA & Verification

Dimensional and structural checks against the design before it leaves the floor.

06

Ship & Handoff

Crated and shipped on schedule, ready for your team — or our partner Rackworx — to populate and cable.
