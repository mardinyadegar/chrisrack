Company

We build the racks that run the data center.

Coreframe Systems started in 2013 as a two-bay rack integration shop outside Austin, Texas. A little over a decade later we're a full rack integration house serving hyperscalers, colocation providers, and enterprise IT teams across the country — still run by data center people, not a holding company.

History

From one custom build to full rack deployments

Coreframe was founded by a small group of data center technicians who were tired of racks arriving from vendors partially cabled, mislabeled, or dead on arrival. The first shop was a single staging bay and a folding table for cable maps. Today that same integration-first culture runs a 42,000 sq ft facility that ships several hundred racks a year, from single 42U cabinets to full-row hyperscale deployments.

We never grew past the point where the technician who built your rack can walk the floor and check on it. That's a deliberate limit, not an accident.
