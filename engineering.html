Engineering

Elevations from people who have to build what they draw.

Our engineering team sits forty feet from the integration floor. That proximity keeps designs buildable, keeps power and cooling math honest, and keeps rack reviews from becoming a bottleneck at the end of the job.

Design Services

Bill of materials to rack floor, without translation loss

We take a target workload, a legacy elevation, or a fully released design package and turn it into a rack build that a technician can actually cable — the first time.

  • Rack elevation & U-space planning
  • Power & cooling load calculations
  • Cable schematics & port mapping
  • Weight & floor-loading review
  • Bill-of-materials sourcing & lead-time management
  • Factory acceptance test (FAT) documentation
Tools & Standards

Industry-standard tools, plant-specific standards

We work inside your data center's naming and cabling standards when you have them, and propose ours — built around standard rack elevation and DCIM-compatible documentation — when you don't.

Legacy Design Cleanup

Redlined or hand-marked elevations redrawn to current standards before a single cable gets run.

Standardized Templates

Reusable rack elevation and cabling templates for recurring pod designs cut design time on repeat orders.

Change Control
