Engineering

Elevations from people who have to build what they draw.

Our engineering team works a few steps from the fabrication floor. That proximity keeps designs buildable, keeps power and thermal math honest, and keeps design reviews from becoming a bottleneck at the end of the job.

Design Services

Workload to enclosure, without translation loss

We take a target workload, a legacy elevation, or a fully released design package and turn it into a cabinet a fabrication team can actually build — the first time.

  • Rack & cabinet elevation planning
  • Structural & seismic load calculations
  • Power & thermal load calculations
  • Cable pathway & containment design
  • Weight & floor-loading review
  • As-built documentation
Tools & Standards

Industry-standard practices, plant-specific execution

We work inside your facility's naming and cabling standards when you have them, and propose ours — built around standard rack elevation and DCIM-compatible documentation — when you don't.

Legacy Design Cleanup

Redlined or hand-marked elevations redrawn to current standards before fabrication starts.

Standardized Templates

Reusable elevation and layout templates for recurring cabinet designs cut design time on repeat orders.

Change Control
