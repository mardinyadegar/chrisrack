Manufacturing

One enclosure. Every discipline, coordinated.

Building a cabinet that's ready for high-density AI compute means structural, thermal, and power design all have to agree before metal gets cut and fabrication begins.

Our Process

Six stages, one schedule

The same released schedule governs engineering and fabrication, so a change on the drawing doesn't quietly become a surprise on the ship date.

01

Design Intake

Once the requirements are agreed upon, the rack configuration is created to meet the specifics.

02

Engineering Release

Once the configuration is decided, engineering begins to assemble the 3D model of all the required parts and a 2D drawing with a parts list is sent out for approval.

03

Fabrication

With a complete engineering documentation package in hand, fabrication can begin as the documents are released to manufacturing.

04

Finishing & Assembly

Fabricated parts are created, then assembled into the finished enclosure exactly as released on the approved drawing.

05

QA & Verification

Dimensional and structural checks are verified according to the design.

06

Ship & Handoff

No surprises on delivery. We ensure that all the specifications and items demonstrated in the documentation are met.
